A coffee cup your guests take home

A 16oz cup with a sleeve that turns. Spin it and Kilkenny landmarks appear in the window. Point a phone at it and they come to life.

How it works

  1. Spin the sleeve. The gate opens onto a painted Kilkenny street.
  2. Scan the cup. The landmarks rise out of it in 3D on the phone.
  3. Keep it. The guest takes the cup with them as a small souvenir.

Guests take it home as a memory of Kilkenny and start their own collection.
